    Making it feel like Destiny

    Research notes behind this version, and what each finding changed on screen. This is a fan project: no Bungie art, logos or fonts are used. Every model is built from simple shapes in code.

    HUD layout

    Destiny 2 keeps the radar top-left. Health and buffs sit bottom-left, with buff icons above the health bar, and the Final Shape redesign put helpful effects on the left and debuffs on the right. This HUD follows that layout, with timers and fill meters on each effect.

    Ludo.guide · KeenGamer

    Boss bar

    Destiny 2 splits tougher enemies' health bars into thirds, because thresholds often signal a new phase. Bosses get a yellow bar with a skull. The bar here is yellow, split into thirds, and has a white "chip" trail that lags behind damage.

    Game Developer · Pro Game Guides

    Typography

    Bungie hasn't published its UI typeface, and fan claims conflict. The in-game type reads as a clean, neutral grotesque. Hanken Grotesk is a free face in that family. Here it's set in light, widely spaced capitals for announcements and semibold for labels.

    Made Good Designs

    Vex materials

    Vex are built from a metal that looks like hammered brass. Their minds are a milky suspension of radiolaria held in an exposed core in the abdomen, and they have triangular heads with a single red eye. Atheon here has a brass body, a glowing white core that flares during damage, and a red eye.

    Destinypedia: Vex

    The Vault itself

    The Vault sits deep inside Venus. Sync plates feed transfer gates into the past and future, and Vex spires rise as pillars of white energy. That's why there are glowing white spire columns, plate-linked gate arches, and a chamber tinted by era: green-gold for Venus (the past), rust-red for Mars (the future).

    Destinypedia: Vault of Glass · Spire

    Oracle layout

    Atheon's Oracles spawn at six spots. 1, 2 and 3 are up the hall toward Atheon: on the left wall, on the middle stairs, and on the right wall. 4, 5 and 6 are by the Mars gate, at the front of the DPS platform, and by the Venus gate. Placement here follows two community encounter maps, including TheRedDelilah’s Atheon map. Three Oracles spawn per wave, and there are three waves. The most popular callout scheme reads the back row left to right, then the front row: 1 2 3 back, 4 5 6 front. Some teams number clockwise instead. Agree on yours before the pull.

    Bungie.net callout poll · Game Rant · Kujay (clockwise)

    Light and glow

    Destiny lights its scenes with strong emissive sources against dark, foggy spaces. This version uses Three.js with an Unreal-style bloom pass, exponential fog, and drifting radiolaria particles. Only the Oracles, the core, the plates and the spires glow, so they stand out as the things you need to look at.

    three.js r160 + UnrealBloomPass (MIT)

    How far this can go

    Each step closer to the real game costs more effort. These estimates are for one experienced developer.

    This page

    Current three.js. Reflective metal and glass, a live mirrored glass floor, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, cinematic color grading, light shafts and low fog. A particle system for teleports, blasts, Oracle shatters and cleanses. Animated Vex and Guardian rigs. Synthesized audio, including a distinct tone for each Oracle spot. Quality adjusts itself to your device, or you can set it.

    done
    Accurate map

    The arena is traced from two community encounter maps. They provide the floor outline, Atheon’s position on the main floor, the raised top room with its pillars and the teleport spawn, the wide stairs stepping down through the hall, all six Oracle spots, the angled Mars and Venus gates, the raised DPS platform with its diagonal stairs, the sunken sync-plate level reached by side stairs, and the rally banner at the entrance. Bots and the simulation use the same floor plan, so walls and stairs are real for everyone.

    done
    Modeled assets

    Low-poly Vex, Guardian and arena models made in Blender and exported as glTF, with baked lighting and real glass and reflection materials. Biggest visual jump.

    3–6 weeks
    Third-person camera

    Over-the-shoulder view, so each seat sees only what that player would see. Most authentic, but slower to learn from than the overhead view.

    3–4 weeks
    Licensed look

    Real Destiny fonts, icons or models need permission from Bungie. Fan projects can't ship them.
